PRESSURE WASHING FAQ

Straight answers
before we spray.

Will pressure washing damage my siding?

Not when the correct method is used. We use a lower-pressure soft-wash approach on siding and reserve stronger pressure for appropriate hard surfaces.

What can you clean?

House exteriors, driveways, sidewalks, patios, decks, garage floors, gutter faces, and other approved exterior surfaces.

Can every stain be removed?

Organic growth and general buildup usually respond well. Rust, oil, oxidation, paint, and deeply embedded stains may require specialty treatment and cannot always be fully removed.

Do I need to be home?

Often, no. We confirm water access, closed windows and doors, gate access, pets, and any property-specific instructions before arrival.
