How Often Should Gutters Be Cleaned in Central Kentucky?

A seasonal gutter-cleaning schedule for Lexington-area homes, including trees, downspouts, and warning signs.

Start with spring and fall

Many homes benefit from inspection and cleaning around spring growth and after fall leaf drop. Properties beneath mature trees may need more frequent attention.

Watch the downspouts

Removing visible debris is only part of the job. Downspouts should move water freely away from the structure.

Signs you should not wait

Overflow during rain, plants growing from gutters, sagging, staining, and water collecting near the foundation deserve attention.

Make it predictable

A recurring seasonal plan can keep gutter care from becoming an emergency.
