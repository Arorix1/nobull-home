Soft Washing vs. Pressure Washing: What Does Your Home Need?

Why siding and roofs need a different cleaning method than driveways, patios, and sidewalks.

The method should match the material

Pressure is useful on appropriate hard surfaces. Soft washing relies on a suitable cleaning solution and low-pressure application for materials that could be damaged by aggressive spraying.

Where soft washing belongs

House siding, painted surfaces, stucco, and roofing commonly need a controlled low-pressure approach.

Where controlled pressure belongs

Driveways, sidewalks, patios, and approved concrete surfaces can often be cleaned with measured pressure and even surface-cleaner passes.

The question to ask

Ask how the company chooses the method for each surface and what it does to protect the property.
