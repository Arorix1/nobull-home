How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Lexington, KY?

A practical guide to local pricing, property variables, and what should be included in a professional exterior-cleaning quote.

The short answer

Residential exterior-cleaning prices depend on surface, square footage, access, buildup, and treatment required. A small concrete area and a complete house-and-driveway reset should not carry the same price.

What changes the price

Stories, steep grades, difficult water access, oxidation, oil or rust, heavy organic growth, delicate materials, and unusually large concrete areas can change scope and labor.

Pressure versus soft washing

Professional cleaning is not about maximum pressure everywhere. Siding and roofs generally require a lower-pressure treatment, while suitable concrete can receive controlled pressure.

How to compare quotes

Ask what surfaces are included, how landscaping is protected, and whether the quoted price can change without your approval.
